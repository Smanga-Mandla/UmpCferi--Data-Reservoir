# UMP CFERI Data Reservoir — Requirements Traceability

## Baseline

- **Source:** System Requirements Specification dated 20 October 2026.
- **Naming:** This project uses **UMP CFERI** as instructed. The former organisation name in the SRS is intentionally not carried into project documentation.
- **Scope:** This document records the SRS functional requirements and their original IDs and priority classifications. Priority meanings are unchanged: **M** = must have in v1, **S** = should have, **C** = could have.
- **Current state:** SRS requirements are tracked against implementation and verification. Phases 1–8 are implemented to the extent noted below. Phase 9 reporting, exports, and audit have passed focused and full-suite verification in the isolated test environment; open metric and privacy rules remain documented rather than assumed.
- **Phase 10 acceptance:** The detailed acceptance matrix below is authoritative for each requirement's required status vocabulary, test evidence, and remaining issues; earlier phase summaries retain their historical implementation notes.

## Cross-cutting project constraints

These constraints come from the project instructions and do not replace or reclassify SRS requirements:

- Use Django templates and Bootstrap, PostgreSQL, and Django migrations.
- Apply deny-by-default access control and retain historical data rather than overwriting it.
- Use environment variables for secrets and never use real CFERI personal or business data in tests.
- Add automated tests for implemented requirements; run checks and tests after each implementation phase.
- Record unresolved requirements in [OPEN_DECISIONS.md](./OPEN_DECISIONS.md).

## Phase 9 implementation notes (technical safeguards, not CFERI policy)

- Business dashboard queries use the user's report and underlying record grants. Board-only users receive no incubatee-level rows or founder identities; all impacted metrics and demographic cells below a provisional minimum population of five are suppressed. The threshold and complementary-suppression policy require CFERI approval (OD-34).
- Reports use the selected observation-period date range and show a monthly jobs/funding trend for historical comparison. Revenue growth is a mean per-business percentage from the earliest saved baseline snapshot to the latest recorded monthly revenue in the selected period, excluding missing/zero baselines. Jobs and funding sum recorded observations in that period. Missing observations remain distinct from recorded zeroes. These are provisional, not approved CFERI definitions (OD-07, OD-31, OD-33).
- Demographic outputs count active linked founders and classify youth using a provisional under-35 threshold as of the current date. This has not been approved; no row-level founder data is serialized to Board report templates, exports, or the API.
- XLSX/PDF exports cover the impact report, role dashboards, interaction list, primary module lists (incubatees, check-ins, partners, MOUs, stakeholders, and documents), expiry/renewal alerts, and the audit log. Sensitive related records are rechecked before serialization; Board-only interaction views/exports omit participant names and free-text topics/outcomes pending the personal-data policy decision (OD-17). Export columns are constrained to fields shown in authorized list/report screens; additional fields or export scopes require CFERI policy confirmation (OD-12, OD-35).
- The monthly summary runs on the first of each month at 07:00 (configured timezone), covers the previous calendar month, and emails each active Admin/Board account separately using that account's reporting scope. This is a technical schedule; SMTP, Redis worker/Beat, and delivery ownership require configuration (OD-04).
- Audit signal coverage applies to ordinary saves/deletes and login, logout, and role changes. Bulk database operations bypass Django model signals and must not be used for audited business changes unless they emit explicit audit events. Audit records are append-only through application model/queryset methods; production database-owner tamper resistance needs database-level operational controls.

## Phase 7 implementation notes (technical defaults, not CFERI policy)

- Monthly check-in dispatch runs daily and sends links for the preceding month starting on configurable day 1; the configurable technical due day is 15. Reminders are disabled unless configured. The 30-day token lifetime is configurable. These are not confirmed CFERI schedule, deadline, or reminder policies (OD-05, OD-30).
- Links contain high-entropy single-use tokens; only token digests are stored. The public form is scoped to one check-in, expires, and cannot be reused. Bank statements and other evidence are stored privately and retrieved through authorization-checked endpoints.
- Observations preserve missing, zero, and not-applicable states and are unique by incubatee, period type, period start, and metric. Submitted check-ins are not silently duplicated.
- Completeness currently treats recorded and not-applicable values as complete across the 14 monthly prompts. Revenue growth, jobs created, funding received, completion rate, mentoring hours, 12-month survival, and cost-per-job calculations are technical provisional definitions, not official reporting policy (OD-07, OD-31).
- Quarterly tax/CIPC statuses and mentor progress ratings are recorded as text without an imposed vocabulary or scale. Exit follow-ups are generated for six and twelve months and submitted through authenticated internal forms; external survey delivery is not implemented.
- The profile timeline includes the available incubatee, check-in, quarterly review, milestone, mentoring, authorized interaction, exit/follow-up, and document events.

## Phase 8 implementation notes

- Interaction list, detail, filters, create/edit, participant relationships, linked incubatees/partners, follow-up tasks, and event/workshop attendance are guarded by the seeded `interactions` role grants. Staff access follows assigned interaction records; Freelancers can access their own permitted interactions; Board Members are read-only; System Admin has no interaction grant in the current matrix.
- Each interaction requires a date/time, type, and nonnegative duration in minutes. A zero-minute duration is accepted; CFERI should confirm whether duration may be omitted or any type-specific duration rule applies (OD-32).
- Existing Stakeholder records are linked where selected. Names for people not yet in the directory can be recorded directly on the interaction/attendance without creating an account or a duplicate person record.
- Interaction field changes create immutable snapshots; deletion is soft deletion. Follow-up tasks are linked to their interaction and task owners must have permission to read that interaction.
- Event/workshop attendance supports Registered, Attended, and Absent. Follow-up task states are Open, In progress, and Completed. These are technical choices, not confirmed CFERI workflows (OD-32).
- Contact details in linked stakeholder records use the existing field-level policy. Board-only interaction views and exports withhold participant names, attendance, follow-up tasks, and free-text topics/outcomes pending CFERI's decision on permitted Board details (OD-17). Other roles remain subject to interaction, stakeholder, and linked-record permissions.

## Phase 10 revalidation notes (22 October 2026)

- Dashboard figures are calculated from authorized stored incubatee, MOU, document, check-in, and metric records. No real or demonstration business figures are seeded by application migrations; synthetic values found by the review are confined to automated tests.
- Business-model relationships use explicit foreign keys or many-to-many relations. Protected foreign keys preserve related records; historical snapshots and time-series records are append-only through application model/queryset APIs. Ordinary authorized deletion is soft-deletion with a 90-day recovery window.
- Per the user-selected interim direction recorded in OD-02, permanent deletion is disabled centrally for all roles, including Super Admin, until a retention schedule is approved. The permanent-delete helper and model deletion path both deny the operation; regression tests verify this behavior. This interim restriction takes precedence over the SRS Super Admin permanent-delete privilege until the approved schedule is available.
- Authorization, privacy suppression, and data source checks passed the automated suite, but a live PostgreSQL relationship/migration run was not available. The matrix’s “tested” status does not imply production integration verification.
- STK-04 assignment relationships now use append-only assignment rows: ending an assignment records actor/time, and historical rows cannot be updated/deleted through normal model/queryset APIs. The profile exposes only active assignments; access to each linked incubatee is rechecked before display and assignable options require per-record UPDATE permission.
- INC-06 was exercised end-to-end through task execution, local email capture, secure link retrieval, and form submission. SMTP delivery and scheduled execution through Redis/Celery Beat remain external deployment checks because those services are unavailable locally.
