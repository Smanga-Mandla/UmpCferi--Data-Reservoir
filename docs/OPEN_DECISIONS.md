## Phase 3 implementation notes (provisional; not CFERI policy decisions)

- DOC-05 alerts are generated by a daily Celery Beat task scheduled for 06:00 in the configured Django timezone (`Africa/Johannesburg`). The 60-, 30-, and 7-day triggers use inclusive calendar-date comparisons. These assumptions remain subject to OD-06 confirmation.
- Dashboard alerts are persisted even when email recipients are not configured. The missing configuration or a delivery failure is recorded on the alert and logged; unsuccessful email delivery is retried on a later scheduled run.
- Email recipients are supplied through `DOCUMENT_EXPIRY_ALERT_RECIPIENTS`; CFERI must confirm who owns the operational recipient list and SMTP configuration under OD-04.
- No document category is reported as missing until CFERI defines which categories are required and under what conditions (OD-06).

## Phase 4 implementation notes (provisional; not CFERI policy decisions)

- MOU expiry reminders use the configured `Africa/Johannesburg` timezone and a daily Celery Beat run at 06:15. The 90-, 60-, and 30-day thresholds use inclusive calendar-date comparisons; task retries preserve dashboard alerts and retry unsent email.
- Email delivery is configured through `MOU_ALERT_RECIPIENTS` and the shared SMTP settings. CFERI must confirm the operational recipient list under OD-04 and OD-11.

## Phase 6 implementation notes (provisional; not CFERI policy decisions)

## Phase 7 implementation notes (provisional; not CFERI policy decisions)

- The daily check-in task uses configurable technical defaults: send from day 1 for the previous month, due on day 15, no reminders unless configured, and 30-day token expiry. These are not approved operational policy (OD-05, OD-30).
- Check-in links are single-use, token-digest-backed, and scoped to one incubatee and reporting period. Dispatch and reminder emails use the configured SMTP backend; delivery is retryable and surfaced in task logs, but live SMTP is not verified.
- Metric formulas use the original baseline snapshot and recorded period observations; completeness counts recorded and not-applicable fields among 14 prompts. Job counts are gross jobs-created observations and do not subtract losses. Funding is reported as recorded funding received. Survival is the share of submitted 12-month follow-ups marked Operating. Cost per job divides programme costs by recorded jobs-created observations for incubatees currently assigned to that programme. These definitions, cohort/time windows, and missing-data treatment need approval (OD-07, OD-31).
- Quarterly tax-clearance/CIPC statuses and mentor progress ratings accept text and are not interpreted until CFERI supplies controlled vocabularies/scales (OD-31).
- Exit follow-up records are generated six and twelve months after the recorded exit date and are collected using authenticated internal forms; external delivery, respondents, and approval remain open (OD-30).
- The profile timeline is chronological and includes incubatee-linked interactions visible to the current user, plus the available incubatee baseline, check-in, review, milestone, mentoring, exit/follow-up, and document events.
